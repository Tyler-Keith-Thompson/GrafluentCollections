Explore GrafluentCollections

Watch data structures work. Every page runs the real library in your browser, one step at a time.

Most fast collections are trees or graphs inside. A sorted set is a search tree that rotates to stay shallow. A spell checker’s dictionary is a tree of shared prefixes. A text index is every suffix of the text, sorted. The shape is what makes them fast, and it is usually hidden.

Each page takes one structure. It says what the structure is for and what else could do the job. Then it lets you run its operations, one step at a time, on a small example you can change, and draws the shape that results. Every page runs the real GrafluentCollections library in your browser, so what you see is what your code would do.

How the pages work

  • What it’s for, and when to choose something else.
  • A small example you can edit.
  • Step, play, go back, or drag the timeline. Each marble on the timeline is one operation.
  • The idea: why it works.
  • Where it breaks: the workload that hurts it, and what to use instead.
  • In Swift: the exact code the page is running.
